# ![Neural Analog]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/neuralanalog/wordmark-on-dark.png){: width="320" }

[Neural Analog](https://neuralanalog.com) is the audio platform I am working on now: a GPU-powered online studio for audio restoration, stem separation and mastering.

[Try it for free](https://neuralanalog.com){: .btn .btn-primary } [Download the VST plugin](https://neuralanalog.com/download){: .btn } [Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/sKaKmZwBQ9){: .btn }

![Spectrogram of an old mono recording before and after Neural Analog restoration]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/neuralanalog/before-after.jpg)

It helps musicians, producers, AI music creators, podcasters, and audio engineers turn old muffled recordings into clean, upscaled, release-ready audio. Upload a file or import from an AI music platform, restore missing frequencies, split stems, clean up artifacts, master the result, and export production-ready audio.

The models run on GPUs in the cloud, so heavy processing needs nothing more than a browser.

{% include youtubePlayer.html id="CPpeacb3cK4" %}

## What it does

- **Audio restoration**: regenerates the high frequencies lost to MP3 compression and old recording gear, for compressed, damaged or AI-generated tracks.
- **Podcast and voice cleanup**: removes background noise, reverb and clipping from speech.
- **Noise removal**: takes background noise, crowd noise and birdsong out of field and live recordings.
- **Suno and Udio cleanup**: removes hiss and artifacts from songs generated with Suno and Udio.
- **Stem separation**: splits a song into vocals, drums, bass, guitar and other instruments, or the instrument you describe.
- **Mastering**: brings a track to streaming loudness, for louder, cleaner releases.
- **Mono to stereo, Dolby Atmos and 5.1**: turns mono recordings into wide stereo mixes, and spreads a stereo song across a surround mix.
- **MIDI transcription**: turns stems into MIDI for any DAW.

## The studio

Compare the original and the processed versions, split stems, and chain the tools into pipelines.

![The Neural Analog studio with separated stems]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/neuralanalog/studio-tracks.webp)

Each stem gets its own track, to solo, mute, process again or download.

![Vocals, bass, drums and other instruments, separated]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/neuralanalog/stem-tracks.webp)

Run a pipeline on a whole album or playlist at once: batch processing is made for labels, YouTube channels, AI creators and DJs.

![Batch processing several tracks with one pipeline]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/neuralanalog/batch-processing.webp)

## Works where you do

- **In your browser**: the online studio.
- **In your DAW**: the [VST plugin](https://neuralanalog.com/download).
- **In Claude and ChatGPT**: the [MCP server](https://neuralanalog.com/mcp).
- **In your code**: the [REST API](https://neuralanalog.com/api-docs).
