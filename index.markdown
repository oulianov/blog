# Nicolas Oulianov

I build AI products by programming and talking a lot.

![Nicolas Oulianov]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/face_crop_hq2.png){: width="200" }

- 2025- I'm building [Neural Analog](https://neuralanalog.com), a platform for creatives to improve audio quality with GPU-based models.
- 2023-2025 - **Founding Engineer** (first employee) at [phospho (YC W24)](https://phospho.ai/), an AI startup. I accelerated the adoption of the latest AI technologies in Paris and San Francisco. We built [the open source platform phosphobot](https://docs.phospho.ai/welcome) to train AI models ([VLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vision-language-action_model)) that control robots.
- 2022-2023 - **Data Scientist** at [QuantumBlack, AI by McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/how-we-help-clients). I helped large companies in Europe progress on GenAI, future mobility, and business analytics.
- 2021-2022 - **Co-founder** of [Ynstant](https://www.ynstant.io/), a carpooling startup. Best memory: building an app from scratch and getting into someone's car thanks to it. Stopped by student loans.
- 2015-2022 - **Double degree in business and engineering** from [HEC Paris](https://www.hec.edu/en) and [Télécom Paris](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/en/home). French [“prépa classes”](https://aphec.fr/wp-content/uploads/2012/03/Introducing_The_PREPA_Classes_in_THE_FRENCH_HIGHER_EDUCATION_SYSTEM.pdf) survivor. Awesome internships: [Criteo](https://www.criteo.com/) AI Lab (online ads R&D), [Foundamental](https://www.foundamental.com/) (Berlin construction VC fund), and [Skillup](https://www.skillup.co/) (Paris startup).

I made this blog to share my stories and the cool stuff I learned in data science, programming, and mathematics.
